The Strata engine keeps every routed expert of a mixture-of-experts model in a tiered store: a small VRAM cache seeded from a routing profile, a pinned host arena holding most of the experts, and the model file itself for the rest. A decode step routes each token to a handful of experts, and the tier serves them from whichever tier holds them, overlapping the misses with the dense work. Getting the seed order right is the difference between a cold cache and a warm one, which on this machine is the difference between roughly two and twenty tokens per second.
